A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) bounty entry: one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The entry

> Four billion years of listening to your wishes, and not one of you ever wished me a good night back.

100 characters, one line — wry, old, and a little tired of being wished upon.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, after a self-check gate enforces the competition's sealed gate: 1–240 characters, a single line, not only white space — then reads the file back and verifies it byte-for-byte.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
